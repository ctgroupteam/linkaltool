Continent Core ERP

Next-generation cloud ERP orchestrating production scheduling, bill of materials (BOM), real-time inventory, and multi-facility procurement.

  • Multi-plant manufacturing workflows
  • Integrated GST e-Invoicing & e-Way Bill
  • Automated audit & compliance trails

Continent Logix TMS

Transportation and logistics management engine with real-time GPS fleet tracking, automated route dispatching, and digital proof-of-delivery (e-POD).

  • Live IoT telematics & speed monitoring
  • Automated freight billing & fuel tracking
  • Driver mobile companion application

Continent HealthSuite

Comprehensive Hospital Information Management System (HIMS) and Electronic Health Records (EHR) compliant with Indian digital healthcare frameworks.

  • ABDM (Ayushman Bharat) Ready
  • OPD, IPD, OT & Lab automation
  • Encrypted patient medical records

Continent FinPulse

Automated corporate treasury, vendor reconciliations, UPI batch collections, and continuous compliance management for growing mid-markets.

  • Multi-bank API integrations
  • Automated 2B/2A GST matching
  • Customizable financial reports

Continent OmniRetail

Cloud Point-of-Sale (POS) and inventory synchronization platform connecting physical outlets, e-commerce, and WhatsApp ordering.

  • Fast 2-second barcode billing
  • Centralized catalog & pricing control
  • Offline billing with automatic cloud sync

Continent Sentinel AI

Edge IoT telemetry and computer vision algorithms for factory floor defect detection, worker safety tracking, and predictive machinery maintenance.

  • Real-time video anomaly detection

  • Automated maintenance work orders
